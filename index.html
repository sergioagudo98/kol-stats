G-ON Stats

G-ON Stats is an internal reporting tool used by the Bitunix KOL team. Partner content creators can authorize it to read their YouTube channel statistics (read-only), so that their weekly performance report is filled in automatically instead of being copied by hand from YouTube Studio.

The tool only reads data. It cannot upload, edit, delete or comment on anything. Creators can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
